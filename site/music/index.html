Questions, answered

How do I start playing music?

Add Vozen Music, join a Discord voice channel and use /play to pick a station.

How do I open the player?

Use /panel to open the Discord player. Use its controls to pause, repeat or leave the call.

Can I listen for free?

Yes. The curated stations and shared player are free. Use /station to switch to another station.

Can others see my likes?

No. The confirmation is only shown to you. Press the heart again to remove your like.

What do Plus and Premium unlock?

Plus gives you track skipping and fewer station limits. Premium extends playback perks to selected servers and adds My Station and shared volume controls. Payments are currently paused.
